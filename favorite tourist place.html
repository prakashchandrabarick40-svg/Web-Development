Taj Mahal - The Crown of Palaces

The Taj Mahal is one of the most beautiful monuments in the world. It is located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India, on the banks of the river Yamuna. It was built by the Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in the memory of his beloved wife Mumtaz Mahal. The building is made of white marble and looks absolutely stunning, especially during sunrise and moonlit nights.

The Taj Mahal is recognized as one of the Seven Wonders of the World and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Millions of tourists visit this place every year from all over the globe. The beautiful gardens, the grand dome, and the four tall minarets make it a masterpiece of Mughal architecture and a symbol of eternal love.
