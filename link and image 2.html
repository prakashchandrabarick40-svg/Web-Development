Shree Jagannath Temple, Puri

The Shree Jagannath Temple in Puri, Odisha, is one of the most revered pilgrimage destinations in India and an essential part of the sacred Char Dham Yatra. Built in the 12th century by King Anantavarman Chodaganga Deva of the Eastern Ganga Dynasty, this majestic temple is dedicated to Lord Jagannath, along with his elder brother Lord Balabhadra and sister Goddess Subhadra. Renowned for its stunning Kalinga architectural style, soaring spire (Shikhara), and deeply spiritual atmosphere, Puri attracts millions of devotees and travelers from around the globe every year.

Rich Heritage and Grand Celebrations

Beyond its divine architecture, Puri is world-famous for its annual Ratha Yatra (Chariot Festival), where the holy deities are drawn in massive, magnificently decorated wooden chariots through the streets of Puri. Another extraordinary specialty of the temple is its sacred Mahaprasad, prepared daily in traditional clay pots inside the world's largest kitchen. Located right along the golden beaches of the Bay of Bengal, Puri offers a perfect blend of spiritual tranquility, historic heritage, and coastal beauty.
