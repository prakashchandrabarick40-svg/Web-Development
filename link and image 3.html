My Favorite Tourist Place

Puri, Odisha

Puri is one of the most famous tourist places in Odisha. It is well known for the Jagannath Temple.

Puri is also famous for its beautiful sea beach, Rath Yatra festival, delicious local food, and peaceful atmosphere.
